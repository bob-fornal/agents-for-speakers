# CodeMash Tracks — What Got Accepted (2026)

For each of the 13 technical tracks: size, AI share, workshop count, themes, style, and representative accepted titles. **(P)** = Pre-Compiler (4 hr), **(W)** = 2-hour workshop.

---

## AI/ML — 18 (17 AI) · 9 workshops
**Themes:** LLM internals and fine-tuning; vector databases and embeddings; evaluation and observability for agents; coding agents and craftsmanship; OWASP Top 10 for LLMs; building a copilot in C#; AI in the browser with JavaScript; multi-agent orchestration (LangGraph.js, event-driven).
**Style:** demystifying and hands-on. Half the track is workshops. Game and film themes are common (D&D, Star Wars).
- Software Craftsmanship for Coding Agents: Taming the Dragon (W)
- Do you want to build a copilot? Star Wars edition! (P)
- Dungeons & Debugging: Observability, Evaluation, and Analytics battle AI Complexity
- LLM Lockdown: Defending Against OWASP's Top 10 AI Nightmares
- AI in the Browser: Practical ML with JavaScript
- Agents That Build Themselves: How to Outlive the Hackathon

**To stand out:** most of this track teaches AI from the basics up. A talk with production evidence or a failure story is rare here.

## Programming Principles — 14 (2 AI) · 6 workshops
**Themes:** fundamentals (assembly, algorithms, quantum, the actor model); Git recovery and configuration; dev containers; API design for the long term; code archaeology; anti-patterns; NASA's Power of Ten; game development with Unity.
**Style:** curious and craft-focused. The workshops are playful ("Hands-On Mystery", "Button Masher to Code Master").
- Assembly Language for People Who Don't Want to Learn Assembly Language (W)
- Mission-Critical Code: What NASA's Power of Ten Can Teach Us
- Git Configuration secrets THEY don't want you to know about
- Digging into the Matrix: Practicing Code Archaeology
- Unlocking Quantum Computing: A Hands-On Mystery (P)

**Style:** relatable and humorous. The workshops are role-play and exercise-driven.

- The Premortem: The Art of Killing a Project to Ensure Its Success

## Career Development — 12 (4 AI) · 3 workshops
**Themes:** resumes and interviews; careers after or alongside AI; unionization; feedback; GTD; yak shaving; career conversations; trust; life after tech.
**Style:** candid, with a hiring-manager point of view and some humor. Talks about AI here are about jobs, not tools.
