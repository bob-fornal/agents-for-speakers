# Momentum Dev Con Trends — 2025 → 2026

Years on file: **2025** (35 sessions, all regular talks) and **2026** (36: 30 full talks + 6 lightning). Consecutive editions.

```bash
python3 tools/score_sessions.py corpus "data/momentum-*.json"
```

## The headline: AI doubled, the voice moved to first person, and the bar rose

| Signal | 2025 | 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sessions mentioning AI | 25% | **53%** | ×2.1 |
| AI term in title | 17% | 42% | ×2.5 |
| First person (I / my) | 20% | **42%** | ×2.1 |
| Numbers / evidence | 11% | **31%** | ×2.8 |
| "practical" | 29% | **53%** | ×1.8 |
| "learn" | 63% | 31% | halved |
| "you'll learn" | 26% | 14% | down |
| Opens with a question | 31% | 11% | down |
| Audience callout | 31% | 17% | down |
| Zero flagged AI words | 66% | **94%** | much cleaner |
| Abstract median | 131 words | 168 (full talks 181) | +37 |
| Title colon | 69% | 58% | down |
| Level majority | Intermediate (54%) | Introductory (58%) | flipped |

**The register changed:** 2025 abstracts read like course descriptions ("you'll learn", questions, audience callouts). 2026 abstracts read like first-person practitioner pitches with evidence.

## Structure changes
- **Formats added in 2026:** Full (45 min), Lightning (5 min), Keynote and Sponsor became explicit Sessionize formats. In 2025 everything was one session type.
- **Topics grew from 14 to 21:** AI split into AI – Dev Tools / Other / Product & Features, Professional Skills into Career Growth + Leadership, and Accessibility, Design & UX, Hardware & IoT and Software Craftsmanship were added.

## Repeats and the circuit
- **No 2025 talk was re-accepted in 2026**; 10 speakers returned with new talks.
- In 2025 Momentum shared 4 identical talks with Stir Trek 2025 and 6 titles with CodeMash 2025. Three of those CodeMash titles came with **rewritten** abstracts (40–65% similar). Some Momentum 2025 talks were also revised when reused later (*MCP Demystified* at KCDC and Stir Trek 2026, about 60% similar; *Coding Effectively with AI* at KCDC and CodeMash 2026, about 80%). Momentum-linked reuses show more revision than the rest of the circuit, where reuse is almost always verbatim.
- Later conferences picked up 2025 Momentum talks: 4 at KCDC 2026, and 2 each at CodeMash 2026 and Stir Trek 2026.

## What to do with this
1. **Write to the 2026 register:** first person, evidence, a stance. "You'll learn" style is fading here.
2. **Don't resubmit last year's Momentum talk.** Bring a new one.
3. **Architecture and non-AI talks have fewer slots than in 2025**, so they need a sharper angle. AI talks face a crowded, higher-scoring family.
