# KCDC Tracks — What Got Accepted (2026)

For each track: size, AI share, recurring themes, title style, and representative accepted titles. Use this to (1) pick the track, (2) check that your title doesn't blend in, and (3) borrow the track's vocabulary.

Counts are 2026 accepted sessions. W = workshop.

---

## Agentic SDLC — 22 (all AI) · newest track
**Themes:** spec-driven development; quality gates and evidence for agent output; keeping human judgment; team adoption blueprints; agent sandboxes; skills/playbooks to steer agents; agents running overnight; BDD/specs as AI input; the hidden cost of generated code.
**Style:** confident stances and confessions. Many first-person experience reports with numbers.
**Accepted titles:**
- The Spec Is the Hard Part
- From Vibes to Evidence: Quality Gates for Coding Agents
- How My Team Got Worse With AI: The Hidden Tax of Generated Code
- Don't Outsource Your Judgment
- Beyond Prompting: How Skills and Playbooks Guide AI Agents
- Coding While You Sleep: How I Deliver Real, Paid Projects Running AI Agents Overnight
- AI-First Engineering with Spec-Driven Development (W)
**To stand out:** real metrics from your own team, a named failure mode, or a reusable artifact (checklist, quality ladder, playbook). Avoid generic "best practices" and tool tours.
