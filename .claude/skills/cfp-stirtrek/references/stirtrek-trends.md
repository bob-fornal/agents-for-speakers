## Category changes
11 categories in 2025 became 9 in 2026, regrouped by outcome:
- *Application Languages, Frameworks & Fundamentals* + *Full Stack Web Development* + *Mobile & Desktop* → **Application Development**
- *Cloud, Infra, DevOps* + *Architecture & Patterns* → **Architecture & Platform Engineering**
- *Data, AI, & ML* → split into **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** (10, all AI) + **Data Engineering & Analytics** (3)
- *Professional Skills* → **Professional Growth & Leadership**
- *Software Quality* → **Software Quality & Delivery**; *Design & Product Strategy* → **Product Design & User Experience**; *Security* → **Security & Privacy Engineering**; *IOT, Home Automation, Maker* → **Other**

## Repeats and the circuit
- **No 2025 talk was re-accepted in 2026**, though 15 speakers returned. Stir Trek, like KCDC, took new talks from returning speakers.
- **But Stir Trek takes talks proven elsewhere on the circuit.** In 2025 it shared **11 talks with CodeMash 2025, all with identical abstracts** (24 shared speakers), plus 4 with KCDC 2024 and 4 with Momentum 2025. In 2026 it shared 6 with CodeMash 2026 and 5 with KCDC 2026.

## What to do with this
1. **Write to the 2026 bar.** Add a payoff line (13% → 38%) and a turn in the hook (19% → 33%).
2. **A talk from earlier on the circuit (CodeMash in January) is a strong Stir Trek candidate as-is**, but a talk Stir Trek already ran last year is not.
3. **AI went from niche to a full category.** Non-AI talks now have fewer AI competitors outside the AI & ML category, but more overall pressure on slots (63 → 55).
