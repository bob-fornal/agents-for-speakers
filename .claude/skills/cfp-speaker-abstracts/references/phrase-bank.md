# Phrase Bank

Recurring phrases from accepted abstracts, grouped by job. Frequencies are from KCDC 2026 (203 sessions) unless marked; **CodeMash 2026 (139)** figures are added where the two conferences differ noticeably. These are **connective tissue** — they make an abstract read as "normal and competent." They never substitute for specifics.

Rule of thumb: use **at most one stock phrase per move**, and surround it with concrete nouns.

## Hook / tension
| Phrase | Use |
|---|---|
| "…is easy to demo and hard to operate / trust" | Demo-vs-production framing |
| "Most <X> talks end at the demo." | Positions the talk past intro content |
| "<X> isn't just <Y> — it's <Z>." (≈11% "not just / isn't just / not about") | Reframe. **Once per abstract at most**; humanize-writing flags repeated use as an AI tell |
| "What happens when…" | Curiosity setup |
| "But what if…" | Turn toward the alternative |
| "the gap between <A> and <B>" | Names the problem space |
| "Let's face it." | Casual, candid opener (use once, ever) |
| "Have you ever…?" / "Ever wonder…?" | Relatable pain. KCDC: better as sentence 2. CodeMash: 20% of abstracts open with a question, and "Ever" is its third most common first word |

| Phrase | Frequency |
|---|---|
| "By the end (of this session/talk/workshop)" | 29 (14%); CodeMash 8% |

## Credibility markers (use only if true)
- "Drawing on <N years / N projects / a real migration>…"
- "Last year, our team…" / "Over the past <N> months, we…"
- "the honest version" / "lessons learned the hard way" / "what we'd do differently"
- "This is not a vendor talk." (if it's genuinely neutral and a sponsor is involved)

## Vocabulary that signals practitioner content
production · real-world · practical · patterns · trade-offs · failure modes · decision criteria · guardrails · observability · lessons learned · in the wild · after the demo · happy path · blueprint · checklist · mental model

## Words that signal fluff (cut or replace with a specific)
leverage · unlock (as filler) · revolutionize · cutting-edge · game-changer · seamless · robust (unqualified) · synergy · "in today's fast-paced world" · "in the ever-evolving landscape" · "it is the age of…" · "deep dive" (unless it truly is) · "and much more!"

The full AI-vocabulary and boilerplate list is in `humanize-writing` (`.claude/skills/humanize-writing/references/signs-of-ai-writing.md`). The words that most often slipped into accepted KCDC abstracts: **robust** (10), **leverage** (10), **enhance** (5), **landscape** (4), **utilize**, **elevate**, **foster** (3 each). Swap them for plain words: *solid / handles X*, *use*, *improve*, *field / space*, *use*, *raise*, *build*.

## Speaker-voice connectors (when a voice skill calls for them)
"So," · "Then," · "Basically," · "Now," · "And," · "But," · a single "…" before a reveal. These read as human and spoken. They beat "Furthermore," and "Moreover," in every CFP. See `references/voice-and-humanize.md`.
