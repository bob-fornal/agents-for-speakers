# Review Rubric

Score every draft before presenting it. Each criterion is 1–5. Anything below 4 gets revised; show the table to the user.

| # | Criterion | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title clarity** | Topic + angle obvious in 2 seconds; 6–12 words | Cryptic pun or bare topic |
| 2 | **Title distinctiveness** | Couldn't be swapped onto a competing talk; similarity to every accepted title is below 0.6 | Generic ("Intro to X", "Best Practices for X") or a clone of an accepted title |
| 3 | **Hook strength** | First sentence ≤ ~15 words, states pain/claim/fact; pivot by sentence 2 | Throat-clearing context |
| 4 | **Specificity** | ≥ 3 named concrete things (tools, patterns, failure modes, numbers) | Adjectives only |
| 5 | **Credibility** | Evidence of real experience in the abstract text | No sign the speaker has done it |
| 6 | **Payoff** | Concrete take-home nouns in the final paragraph | No stated outcome |
| 7 | **Audience fit** | Level and audience clear; matches target track | Ambiguous or mismatched |
| 8 | **Length & shape** | In target word range; ~3 paragraphs; scannable | Wall of text or too thin (< 80 words) |
| 9 | **Human voice** | Sounds like *this* speaker (their voice skill or past writing); 0 flagged AI words; burstiness ≥ 0.35; ≤ 3 triads; "you" and "we'll" | Marketing copy, AI accent, or interchangeable with any speaker |
| 10 | **Feasibility** | Scope fits the time slot; demo plan realistic | Promises mastery of a huge topic in 60 min |

**Ship threshold:** total ≥ 42 / 50 and no criterion below 3.

## Five-second test
Hand someone only the title and first sentence. Can they say (a) what the talk is about and (b) why it's worth an hour? If not, revise those two lines before anything else.

## Reviewer-notes checklist (private field, if available)
- Have you given this talk before? Where? Recording link?
- What's the demo plan and fallback if Wi-Fi fails?
- Why you — what hands-on experience backs this?
- Any vendor affiliation, stated plainly.
- Flexibility: can it be 30/45/60 min, or a workshop?
