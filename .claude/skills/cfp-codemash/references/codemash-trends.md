## Track changes
- **Split:** *AI/ML/Data* (14) → *AI/ML* (18) + *Databases* (7). AI got its own track, and it grew.
- **Shrank:** Security 15 → 8, Software Quality 15 → 8, Architecture 15 → 10. Small tracks swing, so treat their averages as noisy.
- **AI share by track, 2025 → 2026:** AI/ML/Data 8/14 → AI/ML 17/18; Career Development 0/12 → 4/12; Hardware/IoT 0/10 → 4/10; Architecture 1/15 → 3/10.

## Repeats and speakers
- **6 talks from 2025 were re-accepted in 2026**, 5 with word-for-word identical abstracts: *Thinking Architecturally*, *Mad Scientist Lab*, *Scaling from 1 to Infinity* (Actor Model), *Command your IoT Projects from the Terminal*, *The Angular Renaissance*, and *Is Vector Search Right for Me?* (AI/ML/Data → Databases).
- **41 of 2026's 111 speakers** also spoke in 2025.
- Speakers with 2+ sessions: 36% (2025) → 31% (2026).
- Cross-conference: 3 CodeMash 2025 talks also appeared at KCDC 2024 with identical abstracts.

## What to do with this
1. **CodeMash re-accepts proven talks.** Hands-on labs and evergreen fundamentals came back unchanged. A strong prior-year talk is worth resubmitting; KCDC showed no such repeats.
2. **Add a payoff line.** It went from 1 in 6 abstracts to more than 1 in 3.
3. **AI moved out of a shared track into its own** and spread into Career, Hardware and Architecture. Non-AI talks in Web/Front-End and Teams & Collaboration still face no AI competition.
4. **Security and Software Quality shrank by half.** Expect more competition for fewer slots there, or check the next year's track list before choosing.
5. **First-person stories fell to 12%**, so they stand out even more.
